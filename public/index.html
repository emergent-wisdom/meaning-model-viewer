Meaning Model Viewer

The runs to open

A Meaning Model run holds a world, the lives in it and how they change, built as events and processes over time. Open one in three views: the explorer, which zooms from a single day to the model's deep past; the processes, over the story's years; and the landscape, which replays how the model was built.
